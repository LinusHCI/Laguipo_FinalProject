A little clarity before you begin

Good questions. Simple answers.

Starting something new can bring a few questions. Let’s put your mind at ease.

Still curious? Talk to us →
Do I need any experience to get started?

Not at all. We start with the basics and explain things in plain language. Whether you're turning on a computer for the first time or just need a confidence boost, there's a place for you here.

Are the courses really free?

Yes. Every course on this page is free to start and finish.

What device do I need?

A computer, tablet, or smartphone with an internet connection is all you need.

Can I learn in my own time?

Yes. Lessons are bite-sized, and you can pause and pick up where you left off.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. You'll earn a certificate to celebrate each course you complete.

What if I need help during a lesson?

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, by email or a scheduled call.
